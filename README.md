## 🎨 Overview

**Exti** is a lightweight, aesthetic Chrome Extension that replaces your default browser new tab with a dynamic **Liquid Chrome Live Wallpaper** and customizable productivity widgets. 

Designed for speed, beauty, and privacy — built purely with **Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript** without external frameworks or tracking scripts.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 🌊 **Live Animated Wallpaper**: Iridescent Liquid Chrome WebM video loop & Dark Minimal theme.
- 🖼️ **Custom Wallpaper & Media**: Upload your own custom photos or WebM/MP4 live videos.
- 🎯 **Bento Grid Settings**: Sleek, modern Bento-style configuration panel.
- 📐 **Free Drag & Drop Layout**: Move widgets anywhere on your screen with custom layout positioning.
- ✂️ **Built-in Photo Crop Tool**: Crop and adjust photo widgets directly inside the tab.
- 🔒 **100% Privacy & Local Storage**: All data, notes, and tasks stay on your device via `chrome.storage.local`.

## 📥 Installation Guide for Users

Installing Exti is simple and takes less than 1 minute:

### Step 1: Download
1. Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/4rmnd/exti-live-wallpaper-tab/releases) tab on GitHub.
2. Download the latest `Exti-v1.0.0.zip` file (or clone this repository).
3. Extract the ZIP file to a folder on your computer.

### Step 2: Load Extension in Browser
1. Open your browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera).
2. Go to **Extension Management**:
   - Chrome / Brave: `chrome://extensions`
   - Edge: `edge://extensions`
3. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle (usually in the top right corner).
4. Click the **Load unpacked** (Muat yang tidak dikemas) button.
5. Select the **`exti`** folder inside the extracted project.

### Step 3: Enjoy!
Open a **New Tab** (`Ctrl + T` or `Cmd + T`) and welcome your new aesthetic dashboard! 🎉

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security

Exti is built with a **Privacy-First** philosophy:
- ❌ **Zero Analytics & Tracking**: No Google Analytics, no telemetries, no third-party trackers.
- ❌ **No External API Calls**: All scripts execute locally inside your extension sandbox.
- 🔒 **Minimal Permissions**: Requests only `storage`, `unlimitedStorage`, and `notifications`.
