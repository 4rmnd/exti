# Privacy Policy for Exti - Live Wallpaper Tab

**Effective Date: May 28, 2026**

Thank you for choosing Exti - Live Wallpaper Tab ("the Extension"). Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data when you use our Extension.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Exti operates entirely offline within your browser. **We do not collect, transmit, distribute, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, or user information to any external servers or third parties.**

## 2. Local Data Storage
All data generated by your use of the Extension is stored strictly locally on your device using your browser's built-in local storage (`chrome.storage.local` and `IndexedDB`). This includes, but is not limited to:
* To-Do lists and tasks
* Pomodoro timer settings and statistics
* Quick Links and shortcuts
* User settings (language, clock format, layout preferences)
* Custom wallpapers (images and videos)

Because this data is stored locally, only you have access to it. If you uninstall the Extension or clear your browser data, your Extension data will be permanently deleted.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. 

## 4. Permissions Used
To function properly, Exti requires the following permissions, all of which are used solely on your local device:
* **`storage`**: Used to save your settings, tasks, and preferences locally.
* **`unlimitedStorage`**: Used to allow you to save high-quality images and video wallpapers locally (via IndexedDB) without space restrictions.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date".

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy or the Extension, please feel free to open an Issue in this GitHub repository.
